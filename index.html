How this analysis was done (methodology — be transparent)

A Solution Expert shows their working, not just magic numbers.
  • Data: 107,018 public Coursera reviews, each with a 1-5 star label and free-text comment.
  • Sentiment: ratings bucketed as positive (4-5★), neutral (3★), negative (1-2★).
  • Theme extraction: lexicon-based keyword matching against curated positive & negative theme word-lists (e.g. "outdated", "peer review", "clear", "practical"), counted per review.
  • Percentages: theme count ÷ total reviews in that sentiment bucket.
  • Examples: one representative real review selected per theme.
  • Limitation: keyword matching catches explicit mentions; nuanced sarcasm may be missed. A production version would add NLP/embedding-based classification. Honest about the trade-off.
Data source: Coursera reviews dataset — 107,018 real learner reviews with 1-5 star ratings & free-text comments. All figures computed in Python from raw CSV records.
