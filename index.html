Methodology — be transparent

A Solution Expert shows their working, not just magic numbers.
  • Data: 107,018 public Coursera reviews, each with a 1-5★ label + free-text comment.
  • Sentiment: ratings bucketed → positive (4-5★), neutral (3★), negative (1-2★).
  • Themes: lexicon-based keyword matching against curated positive & negative word-lists, counted per review.
  • Examples: one representative real review selected per theme.
  • Limitation: keyword matching catches explicit mentions; nuanced sarcasm may be missed. A production version would add NLP/embedding classification. Honest about the trade-off.
